## Part 1: the technical plan

### What has been added since the first meeting

- Public pages read events, news and fixed pages from Sanity. Editors preview drafts through Sanity's Presentation tool ([0021](../decisions/0021-content-from-sanity.md)).
- Administrators manage the member register, household fees and CSV exports. A household can pay first and add members afterwards ([0025](../decisions/0025-member-register-in-the-application.md)).
- Offers, registrations and member documents are stored and edited in the application. Offer details stay behind member login, as agreed at the first meeting ([0022](../decisions/0022-offers-and-documents-in-the-application.md)).
- Members book volunteer shifts for events. Administrators manage shifts and export bookings ([0024](../decisions/0024-volunteer-shifts.md)).
- The application keeps members' contact details and fee status updated in Brevo and creates mailing drafts for its member list or a selected audience. Administrators test-send and send in Brevo. Failed mail and contact updates are queued for retry ([0026](../decisions/0026-outbox-and-brevo-contacts.md)).

This is an implementation update, not a record of customer acceptance. Before launch, the [system plan](../projektplan.md#week-12-ends-with-a-usable-system-and-a-handover) requires real editors and administrators to use the system, migration of the old site's content, accessibility checks, a backup restore and handover.

### EmDash as an alternative to Sanity

EmDash is an open-source content management system developed by [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/emdash-cms-plugin-registry/) and community contributors. It runs on the association's own server and uses Astro to render the public website. It has WordPress import, draft preview and editing directly on the page. Alexander is looking into it as a promising alternative to Sanity. The [research report](../research/emdash-instead-of-sanity.md) covers the comparison.
