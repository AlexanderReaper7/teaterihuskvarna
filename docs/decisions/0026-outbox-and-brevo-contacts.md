# 0026: An outbox for mail and Brevo, and members as Brevo contacts

The Decision section is the user's. Everything under Agent notes is an agent's.

## Decision

- Mail goes through an outbox: a table the change's own transaction writes to, and a job that retries until the mail is sent. Decided by the user on 2026-09-28, after the GPT-6 Sol review found that a shift reminder was marked as sent before the mail went, so a failed send was never retried.
- Brevo keeps one list with every member who has an account, and each contact carries attributes the association builds its audiences on as segments in Brevo, so it can write and send campaigns in Brevo without going through the application first. The user asked "cant we just keep the lists continuosly updated? so we have one list per audience that also doesnt depend on doing something inside the admin page first", and then chose one list with attributes over one list per audience. Decided by the user on 2026-09-28, after the GPT-6 Sol review found that a new list per mailing ([0023](0023-brevo-list-per-mailing.md)) never gets cleaned up, and Brevo allows 300 created lists.
- The contacts are updated on every change to the register, not by a comparison on a timer. Decided by the user on 2026-09-28.
- The mailing page stays, and creates its draft for the whole list or for a segment instead of a new list. Decided by the user on 2026-09-28.
- Before a draft goes to a segment, the application checks in Brevo that every contact in it is on the members' list, and refuses the segment otherwise. The user asked for safeguards beyond a written instruction: "The administrators might not be very hood with computers, so id like safeguards that are more than just 'follow the guide'", and chose this check alone over also keeping non-members out of Brevo or offering only fixed audiences. Decided by the user on 2026-09-29.
- Preparing a mailing saves it before Brevo is asked for its draft, and puts the mailing's number in the campaign's name, so a draft whose answer is lost is found by that name. The user chose this on 2026-11-06, after the GPT-6.1 Sol review of #208, over keeping the match on the campaign's content with a longer timeout.

## Agent notes

Written by an agent on 2026-09-28. Everything below is a default the user has not reviewed.

### The outbox

- [`Outbox`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/outbox/Outbox.java) writes a row in the caller's transaction and tries it on a background thread after the commit. A failed attempt waits one minute, then twice as long after each further failure, six hours at most. A row is never given up, following the decision above: after 10 attempts, about eight and a half hours, it is logged as an error once and tried every six hours. A row is deleted when its work is done, so the table holds only what is pending, and a row that stays is work that never succeeded, such as an address the relay refuses for good. Until the GPT-6 Sol review on 2026-09-29 the row was deleted after 10 attempts, which lost work after an outage of that length. [`V12__outbox.sql`](../../src/main/resources/db/migration/V12__outbox.sql) has the table.
- Each kind of work, mail and Brevo contacts, has one thread of its own for the attempts after a commit. An attempt holds a database connection while it waits on the other system, so a burst ties up one connection per kind plus the retry job's, and a slow Brevo never delays a login mail.
- An attempt locks its row with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, so the attempt after the commit and the retry job never do one row twice at once, and neither would two instances of the application.
- Work must be safe to repeat, because an attempt can succeed at the other end and still fail here, such as on a timeout. A mail sent twice in that case is the accepted cost.
- A login mail's link sits in the table in plain text until it is sent, normally within a second. Everywhere else only a token's hash is stored ([0015](0015-login-links-on-spring-security.md)). Someone who can read the table can read the other tables too, and the link expires on its own, so the agent judged the difference small.
- [`Mailer`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/login/Mailer.java) keeps its rule that a response must not reveal whether an address is known: the mails that depend on an address are queued from `Background`, off the request thread, so the outbox row costs the request nothing.

### The Brevo contacts

- A contact is keyed by `ext_id`, the member's id, so a changed address moves the same contact and keeps its unsubscribe. The attributes are `NAMN`, `PAID_YEAR` (text holding the latest year the member's fee is paid, by themselves or their household), `LAST_SHIFT` (the day the latest shift they were booked on started) and `OFFERS` (the offers they are registered for, as `;12;15;`). The application creates the attributes on its first call. Everything is in [`HttpBrevo`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/mailing/HttpBrevo.java).
- The audiences [0023](0023-brevo-list-per-mailing.md) named are segments the association saves in Brevo: every member is the list itself, paid this year is `PAID_YEAR` equal to the year, not paid is the opposite, volunteers are `LAST_SHIFT` within the last 12 months, and an offer's registrants are `OFFERS` containing `;<id>;`. The offer's page in the application shows that value. Brevo's API has no call that creates a segment, so they are made by hand, once, and the application offers every segment in the account. A segment must be built on the members' list, or a mailing to it reaches other contacts too, such as an administrator's address on the test list. [`MailingService`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/mailing/MailingService.java) reads every contact in the segment before preparing a draft, a thousand a page, and refuses it if any is off the list; the preview shows how many members a mailing reaches and says why a segment cannot be used. The check is at preparing: the segment can change in Brevo before the administrator presses send there, and a campaign made directly in Brevo is not checked at all.
- Preparing also refuses an audience that reaches no member, the list or a segment, because it holds no one or everyone in it has unsubscribed. In sunsarav's live check on #60 (Verify real Brevo audiences, delivery and unsubscribe handling), Brevo made no draft to a list whose only contact had unsubscribed, and the application showed its retry message. Brevo answers such a draft with 400 `invalid_parameter`, "There are no contacts associated with the given recipients info". On 2026-11-04 the agent got that answer from the development account for an empty list and for a list whose only contact was blocklisted, both at once and 45 s later. A list with one ordinary contact got its draft 15 s after the contact was added, but the same 400 in the first second, so a draft right after a member joins can still fail with the retry message, which is then the right advice. An account that unsubscribes per list also names a contact's unsubscribed lists in `listUnsubscribed`, which the count leaves out too. The application now counts the list too before every draft, one call to Brevo per thousand members, and the preview says that 0 members are reached and offers no draft (#200). [`FakeBrevo`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/mailing/FakeBrevo.java) counts its list as every account in the database, because the development seed and the browser tests write accounts with SQL, which hands the fake no contact, and before #200 its list of 0 still got a draft.
- Brevo can make a campaign and still fail to answer. In the agent's live check on #60 on 2026-11-06, the page showed the retry message while Brevo made the campaign 10 s after the request, the length of the client's timeout. The log never held that campaign, and a retry would have made a second one. So [`MailingService`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/mailing/MailingService.java) saves the mailing in status `creating`, with no campaign, before it asks Brevo, and names the campaign with the day, the subject and the mailing's number, as in `Utskick 2026-11-06 Höstens program #12`. An answer with a campaign makes the mailing a draft. A lost answer leaves it waiting, shown as Väntar på Brevo, and the application looks among Brevo's hundred newest campaigns for that exact name right away, and again each time the log is read or a mailing is prepared. A mailing whose campaign has not shown up after five minutes is deleted as one Brevo never made. A 4xx answer counts as Brevo making nothing, and the mailing is deleted at once.
- A partial unique index in [`V15__mailing_attempts.sql`](../../src/main/resources/db/migration/V15__mailing_attempts.sql) allows one waiting mailing at a time, so a double click, or a retry while Brevo may still make the first draft, gets a message to wait instead of a second campaign. A unique `brevo_campaign_id` keeps two mailings from claiming one campaign.
- A campaign tag would have been simpler, but the development account, on Brevo's free plan, which [0005](0005-brevo-campaign-drafts.md) counts on for the association, answers a tag with 405 `method_not_allowed`. The version of #208 the GPT-6.1 Sol review read matched instead on name, subject, recipients and HTML, and the review found that it missed a retry made before Brevo listed the first draft, a retry across midnight, and a copy made by hand in Brevo with the same content.
- Left open: a campaign Brevo makes more than five minutes after the request stays in Brevo with no mailing in the log. A database reset that starts the mailing numbers over against the same Brevo account could match an old campaign with the same day, subject and number.
- Until #208 the page caught Brevo's failure and logged nothing, so the cause of a failure was lost. Preparing, counting and test sends now log it at WARN with Brevo's answer.
- The member, fee, offer and shift services publish `MemberChanged` in their transactions, and [`BrevoContactChanges`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/mailing/BrevoContactChanges.java) writes an outbox row with the member's id. [`BrevoContacts`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/mailing/BrevoContacts.java) reads the member as they are when the row is done, so a retry never sends an old state. A member who is gone, or has no account, has their contact deleted, so a deleted member's address leaves Brevo too.
- Brevo resubscribes a blocklisted contact whose address changes ([update-contact](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/update-contact)). So the contact is read before each update, the address is sent only when it has changed, and then a contact that is blocklisted gets `emailBlacklisted: true` sent back with it. An unsubscribe that lands between that read and an address change is lost, a window of milliseconds on a change only an administrator makes; closing it would need Brevo to report unsubscribes to the application. The application never unblocks a contact and never blocks one that was not.
- `PAID_YEAR` is text: Brevo ignores a value that does not match an attribute's type, so an empty string could not clear a number when a fee is undone. A numeric version existed only in unpushed commits on 2026-09-28, and no account has run it. Brevo cannot change an attribute's type once made, so an account that had created it as a number would need the attribute deleted by hand first. `LAST_SHIFT` stays a date, for segments such as the last 12 months, and is cleared with `null`, which the reference does not describe; it is cleared only when a started shift is deleted.
- Two outbox rows for one member could run at once and leave the older state in Brevo, so each row takes a PostgreSQL advisory lock on the member for its transaction before it reads the member.
- A household fee changes the contact of everyone in the household: when it is marked, when it is undone, and when a payer who paid it in no household gains one ([0025](0025-member-register-in-the-application.md)). `LAST_SHIFT` changes when a shift starts, with nothing written, so a job reports each shift once it has started, within five minutes, and `volunteer_shift.start_reported` records it.
- [`V13__brevo_contacts.sql`](../../src/main/resources/db/migration/V13__brevo_contacts.sql) queues every member with an account once, so the list starts complete, and drops the per-mailing list columns.
- A mailing no longer knows how many it reaches when it is prepared. The log shows Brevo's count of mails sent instead.
- The list is made once by hand in Brevo and named by `BREVO_LIST_ID`.
- Not verified against a real account: that an empty string clears a text attribute and `null` a date, that Brevo answers 400 for an attribute that exists, and that a date attribute takes `yyyy-MM-dd`. They follow Brevo's API reference as read on 2026-09-28. [`HttpBrevoTest`](../../src/test/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/mailing/HttpBrevoTest.java) checks only the requests the application sends. The run under Verification covered none of them: the attributes did not exist yet, and it involved no shift and no changed address. That `emailBlacklisted: true` sent with a new address keeps the contact blocklisted was checked against a real account in #60, by sunsarav on 2026-10-21 and by the agent on 2026-11-06.

### The mailing preview

Written by an agent on 2026-11-10, with JTE 3.2.5 ([0004](0004-jte-for-templates.md)).

- The preview page shows the mail in `<iframe sandbox src>`, and the mail is a response of its own from `GET /admin/utskick/forhandsgranska/{key}`. Until 2026-11-10 the page carried the mail in the frame's `srcdoc` attribute. JTE 3.2.5 writes nothing for a dynamic `srcdoc`, because escaping cannot make that attribute safe: the browser decodes the attribute and parses the result as a document.
- [`AdministratorMailingPageController`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/web/AdministratorMailingPageController.java) keeps the mail it built in the administrator's session under a new random key, and the frame's address carries the key. The session holds one mail, the latest. A frame whose key is no longer the latest gets 404 and a sentence that says a newer preview replaced it, so a page never shows a mail that its hidden fields would not create. Another administrator, or the same one in another session, gets the same 404.
- The mail stays with the session in PostgreSQL until the next preview or the end of the login. The preview still writes nothing to `mailing`, and asks Brevo only for the segments and the count.
- The frame's `sandbox` attribute allows nothing, so the mail runs no script and has an origin of its own. [`SecurityConfiguration`](../../src/main/java/se/teaterihuskvarna/login/SecurityConfiguration.java) sends the response with `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox; default-src 'none'; img-src https:; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; base-uri 'none'; form-action 'none'; frame-ancestors 'self'` and `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`. The policy does what the attribute does when the address is opened in a tab, where the attribute is absent. `nosniff` and `no-store` are Spring Security's defaults on the administrator chain. Every other administrator response keeps `X-Frame-Options: DENY`.
- The mail carries its styles in `style` attributes and its pictures from Sanity's CDN, which is why the policy lets inline styles and `https` pictures through. A picture from an `http` address would not show in the preview.
- Rejected: a GET with the form in the query string. The administrator's own words can be 5000 characters, and each of å, ä and ö is six characters once percent-encoded, against Tomcat's 8 KB limit on the request line and headers.
- Rejected: posting the hidden form into the frame with `<form target>`. A frame loads that way only after a click or a script, and the preview shows the mail without either.
- Rejected: keeping the form in the session and building the mail again for the frame. The content could change between the two requests, the frame would need its own answer for content that had been unpublished, and `MailingForm` would have to be serialisable for the session's sake.
- `POST /api/admin/mailings/preview` is unchanged: a REST client gets the HTML in the answer to its own POST, and has no frame to fill. That answer carries `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `nosniff` and `no-store`, and not the policy above.
